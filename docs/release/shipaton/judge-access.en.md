## Where to look
- **Free play:** ring screen → "Choose Gem & Depth" → pick any gem, Depth 1. Four mini-games (Glass Break, Glass Trace, Kurukuru World, Crusher Room).
- **Paywall / purchase:** ring screen → "Unlock World 1 Depths 4–6" (also shown when selecting a locked depth).
- **Restore:** paywall or Settings → Purchases.
- **After unlocking:** Depths 4–6 still require clearing the previous depth. The unlock never awards gems; the 20.00 ct gem needs three consecutive S grades at Depth 6.
